Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to Voyage Calculator. We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about how information may be handled when you use our website.

Website: Voyage Calculator.

Our online calculators process the values you enter in your browser. We do not intentionally transmit calculator inputs to our own server or maintain a database of those inputs.

We will update this policy when our use of advertising or other tracking technologies changes. Where required by applicable law, appropriate consent and privacy choices will be provided.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. If Google advertising is used, you may also be able to manage ad personalization through Google's privacy controls.

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. If we add analytics, advertising, or other third-party services, those providers may process information under their own privacy policies. We encourage visitors to review the relevant policies.

Technical information processed by hosting or other services may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain the website.
  • Protect website security and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • Understand website usage if analytics services are added.
  • Deliver and measure advertisements if advertising services are enabled.

7. Children's Privacy

Voyage Calculator is intended for a general audience. We do not intentionally collect personal information from children through our calculator tools.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information. You may also have choices regarding cookies and personalized advertising.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, or legal requirements change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit our Contact Us page.
